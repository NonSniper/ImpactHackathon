# Illustrative photography

The images below are illustrative and do not depict actual CREDEM products. They are used in the browser-demo catalog and, where noted, on the homepage.

- [A plate of cooked rice with stew](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_plate_of_cooked_rice_with_stew.jpg), THE ROSE BOY, Wikimedia Commons, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution is not required; the creator and source are credited here. Used for illustrative Meals products.
- [Vegetable soup and bread at the office](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vegetable_soup_and_bread_at_the_office.jpg), JIP, Wikimedia Commons, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). No alterations beyond display sizing. Used for the illustrative vegetable soup in demo and local test data; attribution appears with the product.
- `sarmale-demo.webp`, `chiftele-piure-demo.webp`, `peste-legume-demo.webp`, and `tocana-legume-demo.webp` are AI-generated illustrations created with the built-in image generation tool for the fictional local and browser-demo catalogs. The sarmale illustration also appears in the homepage hero. Prompts requested realistic home-cooked catalog photography of cabbage rolls, chicken meatballs with mashed potatoes, baked fish with vegetables, and vegetable stew with beans, respectively, in natural daylight without text or logos.
- `coltunasi-congelati-demo.webp`, `legume-congelate-demo.webp`, `chiftele-congelate-demo.webp`, `ardei-congelati-demo.webp`, and `clatite-congelate-demo.webp` are AI-generated illustrative images for fictional frozen products. Prompts requested natural-light catalog photos of frozen dumplings, mixed vegetables, uncooked chicken meatballs, stuffed peppers, and cheese-filled pancakes, without branding or text. The frozen dumplings illustration also appears in the homepage category panel.
- `plan-clasic-cutii-demo.webp`, `plan-vegetarian-cutii-demo.webp`, and `plan-familie-cutii-demo.webp` are AI-generated illustrations made with the built-in image generation tool for fictional meal plans. Prompts requested natural-light photographs of packaged classic, vegetarian, and family meals without branding or text. The family meal-plan illustration also appears in the homepage category panel.
